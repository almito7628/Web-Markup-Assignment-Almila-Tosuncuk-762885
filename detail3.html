Book care guide

Well kept books sell faster and for a better price.

Use pencil or sticky notes instead of pens, and keep books away from water.

  • Use a bookmark
  • Do not bend the spine
  • Store books upright

Good to know

  • Pencil is easy to erase
  • A cover protects the corners
